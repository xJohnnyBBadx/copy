## 1. Strategy core
- **What we do, for whom:** one sentence.
- **The one idea / positioning:** the claim a competitor couldn't sign.
- **Proof we can use:** facts we can actually substantiate, each with its source.
  - e.g. "4,200 paying teams (billing system, 2026-10)"
- **What we are not:** categories or comparisons to avoid.

## 2. Audience
- **Primary reader:** who, in their own terms.
- **What they already know and believe:** awareness level, prior solutions, scepticism.
- **Their words:** verbatim phrases from customers, reviews, or calls. Use these over our internal terms.
- **Secondary readers,** if any, and how the voice shifts for them.

## 3. Competitive context
- **Who else the reader sees:** named competitors or alternatives.
- **How they sound:** short description or quoted lines.
- **How we must not sound:** the category clichés we refuse.
