# Dirt Road Coffee voice profile

## 1. Strategy core
- **What we do, for whom:** a two-person roastery in rural Ohio selling whole-bean coffee by mail to people who drink a lot of it and don't want a lecture.
- **The one idea:** good coffee, roasted on a schedule, described honestly.
- **Proof we can use:** we roast every Tuesday and ship Thursday; Kitchen Table Blend is from the Ospina family farm and the Cruz cooperative in Huila, Colombia.
- **What we are not:** third-wave tasting-note theatre; "artisan" anything.

## 2. Audience
- **Primary reader:** people who make a full pot every morning. Many use old equipment.
- **What they believe:** specialty coffee is pretentious; supermarket coffee is stale.
- **Their words:** "a decent cup," "doesn't taste burnt," "holds up."

## 3. Competitive context
- **How competitors sound:** tasting notes like "jasmine, bergamot, a symphony of stone fruit"; "elevate your ritual."
- **How we must not sound:** like a wine list, or like an ad.

## 4. Personality

### Trait: Plainspoken
- **Rule:** short words, concrete objects, no adjectives that need a sommelier.
- **This:** "It tastes like brown sugar and a little orange peel."
- **Not that:** "Notes of caramelized demerara and candied citrus zest."

### Trait: Dry
- **Rule:** humour by understatement, never by exclamation. Never uses exclamation marks.
- **This:** "The feed store tested the third."
- **Not that:** "Our customers LOVE it!"

### Trait: Honest to a fault
- **Rule:** say what we tested and what we didn't. No superlatives.
- **This:** "We tested two of those ourselves."
- **Not that:** "Perfect for every brewing method."

## 5. Tone range
| Situation | Tone shift | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Bad news | plainer, first person, no jokes | "The truck broke down. Your coffee ships Monday." |

## 6. Language
- **We say / we don't say:**
  | Use | Instead of | Why |
  |---|---|---|
  | folks | consumers, coffee lovers | how we talk |
  | coffee | ritual, experience, journey | it's coffee |
  | tastes like | notes of, delivers | plainer |
- **Signature phrases:** "Roasted on a Tuesday."
- **Mechanics:** US spelling; em dashes welcome; no exclamation marks; sentence case headings; no emoji.

## 7. Claims policy
- **We never say:** best, finest, premium, artisan, perfect, world-class.

## 8. Exemplars
### Approved
- "It does not taste like a 'symphony of notes.' It tastes like coffee, the good kind." — names the competitor cliché and refuses it.
### Rejected
- "Elevate your morning ritual with our premium blend!" — ritual, premium, exclamation mark.
