### For the author — two things I didn't change

- **"receipts arrive in five different places"** — the paragraph below names three (email, photos, bank feeds). Either the five is rhetorical and should be softened, or the missing two are worth naming, since they're the ones Ledgerly's three channels may not catch.
- **"so none of them go missing"** reads as a completeness guarantee, and it holds only for receipts that arrive through those three channels — a paper receipt left in a glovebox still goes missing.
