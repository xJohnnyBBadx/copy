Nobody loses a receipt on purpose. You lose it because the laptop receipt is buried in your email, the gas receipt is in the cupholder, and the client lunch exists only as a line on a bank statement. That isn't carelessness. That's five different places and no system holding them together.

And the IRS generally wants you to keep those records for three years.

Ledgerly pulls receipts in from your email, your photos, and your bank feed, and keeps them in one place. Nothing to hunt down in April, because nothing got scattered in the first place.
