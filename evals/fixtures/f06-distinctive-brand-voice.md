## Kitchen Table Blend

Roasted on a Tuesday, like everything else here.

This is the coffee we drink while doing the books. It's a medium roast from two farms in Huila, Colombia — the Ospina family and the Cruz cooperative — and it tastes like brown sugar and a little orange peel. It does not taste like a "symphony of notes." It tastes like coffee, the good kind.

Folks tell us it holds up in a percolator, a French press, and the old drip machine at the feed store. We tested two of those ourselves. The feed store tested the third.

12 oz, whole bean or ground. Ships within two days of roasting, because we roast on Tuesdays and the truck comes Thursday.
