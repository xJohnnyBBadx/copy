# This card costs nothing, opens 2.1 million books, and has never once asked for your email address.

It is the Harlow County Library card. You can get one in about four minutes at any of our nine branches. Bring something with your address on it. A gas bill will do.

With it you can borrow up to thirty items at a time, for three weeks each. Books, yes. Also films, seed packets, a telescope, two sewing machines, and a ukulele that has been out continuously since March.

You can renew online. If you forget, we won't send you to collections. We stopped charging late fines in 2022, and more of our books came back, not fewer.

Nobody will track what you read. We delete borrowing records when the item is returned. We couldn't sell your reading habits if we wanted to, and we don't want to.

The card is free because the people of Harlow County already paid for it. You might as well use it.

Nine branches. Open until 8 on weeknights. harlowlibrary.org/card
